Help with TapBudget for iPhone and the web version.

Mark a bill paid

Switch the bill on. Today’s date fills in. If you paid on another day or a different amount, change the date and the amount right under the bill.

Install and update

TapBudget for iPhone is shared through TestFlight. Install the TestFlight app from the App Store, open your invite link, tap Accept, then Install. Updates arrive in the TestFlight app. A test version stops working after 90 days, so save a backup file before then and install the newer one.

The web version needs no install. Open it in your browser and add it to your phone or computer.

Keep a backup

Your budgets live only on your device. If you delete the app or reset the phone, they can be lost. Save a backup file now and then, and keep it somewhere safe.

  • Open the menu and choose Save a backup file. It holds everything in the budget, including every payment and its date.
  • To bring a budget back, choose Restore from a file… and pick the file. TapBudget asks first: add it as a new budget, replace one, or merge into it.
  • With more than one budget, Save all budgets in one file appears too. Only the iPhone app reads that file.
  • No file handy? Paste a backup… takes the text of a backup instead.

Move a budget to another phone, or to the web

Nothing syncs on its own. Save a backup file on one device and restore it on the other. The same file works in the iPhone app and in the web version.

Share links, from Share this budget, work only in the web version.

Change the colors and look

Open the menu and choose Wallpaper and colors. Add a wallpaper, choose a flat or glass look, pick a style for the three gauges on Overview, choose how switches look, and pick a ready-made set of colors or your own. Light, dark and automatic are in the menu too. Each budget keeps its own look.

Easy mode (big print)

Open the menu and choose Easy mode (big print) for large text, four big buttons and plain words. Choose Large or Extra large on the More tab. To go back, tap Use the full app on the More tab.

The cash flow chart

Tap Explore the cash flow on Overview, or open Insights. Drag to move, pinch to zoom (or use + and −), and tap a bar for details. If the chart looks too tall or too short, tap Scale and choose Auto, or a height such as $5,000 or $10,000.

Type what your bank app shows under My chequing balance and tap Save my balance. The chart then shows whether you are ahead of or behind plan.

Loans and credit cards

In Settings, add a loan or card and choose the bill that pays it. When you switch that bill on, the balance goes down.

Report a problem

In TestFlight, take a screenshot, then tap Share Beta Feedback. Or email tapbudget17@gmail.com and say what you were doing and what you saw.

Delete your data

Open the menu, choose Manage budgets, and delete a budget. Deleting the app removes its data from your phone.
