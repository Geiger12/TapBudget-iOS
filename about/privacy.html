Privacy policy

TapBudget does not collect your data. Your budgets stay on your device, and there is no account.

  • Saved on your phone
  • No account
  • No tracking

Effective November 28, 2026. Covers TapBudget for iPhone and the web version. App by TapBudget.

What the app stores

TapBudget for iPhone stores what you enter: budget names, bill and income names, amounts, due days, paid dates, the amounts you spend, loan and credit card balances, your starting balance and the chequing balances you type in. It also stores your settings, such as colors, the look you pick, light or dark mode, Easy mode, and a wallpaper picture if you choose one.

All of this is saved in the app’s own storage on your iPhone. Like other app data, it can be included in your iPhone backup, whether that goes to iCloud or to a computer. That backup is yours, under your Apple settings, and we cannot see it.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no sign-up and no login. TapBudget for iPhone has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet to do its work. It does not send your budgets to us or to anyone else.

The links in the app to this privacy policy and the support page open in your browser.

Photos for your wallpaper

If you choose a wallpaper, iOS shows its own photo picker. TapBudget gets only the picture you pick, and never sees the rest of your photos. It shrinks the picture and keeps a copy in its own storage. You can remove it at any time in Wallpaper and colors. Wallpaper pictures are not part of backup files.

Backups and sharing

You choose when to save a backup file. The iPhone share sheet lets you save it to Files, send it with AirDrop or pass it on another way. The file holds your budget as readable text: the items, every payment with its date, loans and cards, and balances. Anyone who has the file can read it, so keep it somewhere safe.

Restoring reads only the file you pick. We do not see backup files.

The web version

TapBudget also runs in a web browser at tapbudget-ashy.vercel.app. That page is hosted by Vercel, which may keep its own technical logs, such as your IP address, under Vercel’s own policy. The web version saves your budgets in your browser, on your device, and does not send them there.

The web version can make a share link. The link contains the budget itself, and your paid history too if you pick that option. Anyone who has the link can read it, so send it only to people you trust. The iPhone app and the web version each keep their own data. They do not sync.

Testing with TestFlight

If you try TapBudget through Apple’s TestFlight, Apple runs TestFlight. Depending on how you join, Apple may share details such as your email address with the developer, along with any feedback or crash reports you choose to send. That is covered by Apple’s terms. TapBudget itself still collects nothing.

Deleting your data

In the iPhone app, open the menu, choose Manage budgets and delete a budget. To remove a wallpaper, use Wallpaper and colors. Deleting the app removes its data from your iPhone. An older iPhone backup you made may still hold a copy until you delete that backup.

In a browser, clearing the site’s data does the same. Backup files and links you shared stay with the people who have them.

Children

The app is not aimed at children, and it collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date.
